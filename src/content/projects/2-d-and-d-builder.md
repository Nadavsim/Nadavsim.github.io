---
title: D&D 2024 Character Builder
summary: A static web app that builds 5th edition (2024 rules) characters and suggests role-based optimizations.
kind: personal
tech: [TypeScript, React, Vite, Vitest]
order: 2
---
Rules data comes from the SRD and cross-checked public sources, with every entry tagged by origin. No backend: characters are saved in the browser and can be exported as JSON.
